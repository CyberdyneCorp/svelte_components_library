Add utility display components: `Timestamp` (live relative or absolute time in a `<time>` element), `HoverCard` (delayed, keyboard-reachable preview card), `ConfirmAction` (click, confirm-dialog or type-to-confirm gating for destructive actions), `CopyableValue` (truncated identifier with icon-only copy), `MaskedValue` (fixed-length mask that keeps hidden secrets out of the DOM, with reveal, auto-hide and copy), `OptionCard` (selectable card around a native radio or checkbox), `FileTypeIcon` and `FileCard` (file tile or row with preview, upload progress, error and remove). `BarChart` accepts multi-series `series` data in grouped or `stacked` layout with a legend and marker shapes. `Dialog` gains `confirmDisabled` and focuses the first body control; `CopyButton` gains `iconOnly` and `copiedLabel`.
