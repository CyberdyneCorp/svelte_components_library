Add `KpiCard` and `BudgetBar`. `KpiCard` is a neutral KPI tile. It conveys trend with an icon plus hidden text, colours by sentiment independently of direction, can render as a link, and has a sparkline snippet. `BudgetBar` is a money budget meter with ok, approaching and exceeded states, a stacked committed amount, overage text, float-free formatting via `formatMoney`, and i18n labels.
